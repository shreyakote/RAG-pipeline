# Week 9 Day 1 — CrewAI Fundamentals

## Objective

Build a multi-agent research system using CrewAI.

## Agents

The project contains three agents:

### 1. Researcher

Researches the assigned topic and collects important information.

### 2. Writer

Converts the research into a clear technical article.

### 3. Reviewer

Reviews the article for accuracy, clarity, completeness and quality.

## Workflow

```text
Researcher
    ↓
Writer
    ↓
Reviewer
    ↓
Final Article
